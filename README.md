# Pixel Planter

A cozy pixel-art mini web game where you tend to your own virtual garden in your browser. Currently in active development—I'll keep adding more plants and features when I'm not busy!

## How to Play
1. Drag a pot into your garden.
2. Plant a marigold seed.
3. Water and fertilize it.
4. Wait for it to grow, then harvest your seeds!
